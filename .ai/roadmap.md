# LaundryOS Product Roadmap

## Selesai (Completed)

- **Sprint 00**: Project Setup & Environment Foundation
- **Sprint 01**: WhatsApp Baileys Gateway Foundation
- **Sprint 02**: Backend REST API Foundation (Customer, Order, DB Migration)
- **Sprint 03**: WhatsApp FAQ Bot (Dynamic Knowledge Base, Matcher)
- **Sprint 04**: Pickup Management & Staff Group Operations (Courier Claim, Weighing Command, SLA Dynamic)

---

## Sedang Berjalan (Active Sprint)

- **Sprint 05: Web Dashboard & POS (Point of Sale)**
  *Konsolidasi Terpadu: Dashboard + Workflow Siklus Cucian + POS Kasir + Laporan Finansial/CRM*
  - ✅ **Milestone 1**: Database Schema, Multi-Item POS, Expenses, Reporting & 2-Way WA Integration
  - ⏳ **Milestone 2**: Frontend Setup, Fresh Modern Layout & Halaman Home (Beranda)
  - ⏳ **Milestone 3**: Walk-in POS Kasir & Order Lifecycle (5-Tab Queue, Status 1-Klik, Bayar, 3 Nota Thermal, Foto)
  - ⏳ **Milestone 4**: Manajemen Pengeluaran Kas Kecil (Petty Cash)
  - ⏳ **Milestone 5**: Laporan Keuangan, Operasional & CRM (Grafik Omset, Piutang, Excel Export)
  - ⏳ **Milestone 6**: Master Settings & Hak Akses Pegawai (Sembunyikan Omset dari Kasir)

---

## Roadmap Mendatang (Upcoming Sprints)

- **Sprint 06: AI Customer Assistant & Guardrails**
  - Google Gemini AI Customer Support di WhatsApp & Web
  - Konteks Bisnis FAN Laundry (layanan, harga, wangi, lokasi)
  - **Strict Guardrails & Intent Boundary**: Menolak dengan sopan pertanyaan di luar konteks laundry
  - Eskalasi otomatis ke admin/owner jika dibutuhkan

- **Sprint 07: AI Conversational Analytics & Business Intelligence (Owner Assistant)**
  - Natural Language Analytics untuk Owner di Web/Mobile Dashboard
  - Tanya jawab data bisnis langsung dari DB: *"Berapa omset dan laba bersih minggu ini?"*
  - Visualisasi grafik otomatis & rekomendasi bisnis cerdas

- **Sprint 08: Loyalty Program, Membership & Customer Retention**
  - Program loyalitas otomatis (Cuci 9x Gratis 1x)
  - Pengingat pintar jadwal cuci pelanggan via WhatsApp (Retention Reminders)
  - Voucher promo & kupon broadcast

- **Sprint 09: Multi-Branch, Inventory & Logistics Scale**
  - Kontrol stok bahan baku (deterjen, parfum, gas, plastik)
  - Skalabilitas cabang baru FAN Laundry & rute kurir multi-armada
