# Sprint 03 - FAQ Bot

## Goal

Automate responses for common customer questions.

## Business Problem

Staff repeatedly answers the same questions every day.

## Scope

- Price Information
- Service Information
- Operating Hours
- Address
- Delivery Coverage
- Pickup Information

## User Stories

As a customer,

I want instant answers,

so I don't have to wait for staff.

## Acceptance Criteria

- FAQ responses work
- Unknown questions are logged
- Response time under 2 seconds

## Technical Tasks

- Keyword matching
- FAQ configuration
- Response formatter
- Logging

## Deliverables

- FAQ Bot

## Out of Scope

- AI
- Orders
- Dashboard

## Definition of Done

- All configured FAQs respond correctly
