# Sprint 09 - Analytics & Business Dashboard

## Goal

Provide business insights for operational and financial decision-making.

## Business Problem

The owner cannot easily monitor business performance.

## Scope

- Daily Revenue
- Monthly Revenue
- Order Statistics
- Customer Statistics
- Top Services
- Repeat Customers
- Operational Summary

## User Stories

As the owner,

I want business insights,

so I can make better operational decisions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Revenue dashboard available
- Customer statistics available
- Order statistics available
- Reports exportable

## Technical Tasks

- Analytics API
- Dashboard Charts
- Report Export
- KPI Widgets

## Deliverables

- Analytics Dashboard

## Out of Scope

- Multi Branch
- SaaS
- Mobile App

## Definition of Done

- Dashboard displays accurate business metrics
