# Sprint 07 - AI Assistant

## Goal

Integrate AI to improve customer support.

## Business Problem

FAQ automation cannot answer every customer question.

## Scope

- AI Integration
- Knowledge Base
- Context Awareness
- Fallback Responses

## User Stories

As a customer,

I want natural conversations,

so I can get answers without waiting for staff.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AI answers unknown questions
- AI uses laundry business context
- AI escalates when necessary

## Technical Tasks

- AI client
- Prompt engineering
- Knowledge base
- Conversation memory

## Deliverables

- AI Assistant

## Out of Scope

- POS
- Membership

## Definition of Done

- AI handles non-FAQ conversations correctly
