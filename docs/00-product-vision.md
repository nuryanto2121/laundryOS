# LaundryOS Product Vision

## Overview

LaundryOS is an internal software platform developed specifically for FAN Laundry Station.

The initial objective is to automate daily laundry operations using WhatsApp.

Instead of replacing every existing tool immediately, LaundryOS will evolve incrementally into a complete Laundry Management System.

---

## Vision

Build a modern, AI-assisted Laundry Management System that reduces manual work, improves operational efficiency, and provides a better customer experience.

---

## Mission

- Automate customer communication.
- Simplify laundry operations.
- Centralize business data.
- Reduce repetitive manual tasks.
- Build a scalable platform for future expansion.

---

## Current Problems

Today the business relies on:

- Manual WhatsApp communication.
- Manual pickup coordination.
- Manual order tracking.
- Manual customer history.
- Third-party POS.

This causes:

- Slow customer response.
- Duplicate work.
- Human error.
- Difficult reporting.

---

## Phase 1 Goal

The first milestone is NOT to build a POS.

The first milestone is to automate WhatsApp communication and laundry operations.

---

## Long Term Vision

LaundryOS will eventually support:

- WhatsApp Automation
- Customer Management
- Order Management
- Pickup & Delivery
- Dashboard
- POS
- QRIS Payment
- Membership
- Inventory
- Analytics
- AI Assistant
- Multi Branch
- SaaS Platform
